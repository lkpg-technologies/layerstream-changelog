## Fixed
- **False offline alerts:** a short internet outage on Layerstream's side could make your screens look offline, and you could get an email saying so even though they were fine. Layerstream now checks its own internet connection before checking your screens. If the connection is down, it waits, and a screen is only reported offline if it stays silent for 10 minutes after the connection returns.

## Changed
- **Fewer emails:** screens that go offline or come back at around the same time are now listed in one email instead of being split across several. To make this possible, a screen can be included up to a minute early, so the email now says "about 10 minutes" and "about 5 minutes".
