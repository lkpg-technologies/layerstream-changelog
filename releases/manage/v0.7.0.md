## Added
- **Offline alerts:** if a screen hasn't checked in for 10 minutes, we email the people on your notification list.
- **Back online alerts:** once the screen has been checking in steadily again for 5 minutes, we email to say it has recovered.
- **One email, not many:** if several screens go offline or come back at the same time, you get one email that lists all of them.
- **Choose who gets alerts:** contact Layerstream support with the email addresses to notify. You can change the list or turn alerts off at any time.
