## Fixed
- **Wide monitors:** on wide or ultrawide monitors, the screen preview in the bundle editor could be so large that the list of screens below it was pushed out of view. The preview now resizes to fit your window, so the screens list is always visible.
- **Browser tab icon:** the browser tab now shows the Layerstream logo instead of a generic globe.
