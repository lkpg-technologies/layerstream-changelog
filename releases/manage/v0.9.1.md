## Added
- **Select like in a photo app:** hover a file and click the circle to select it. Once you've started, click any file to add it, or Shift+click to select a whole range. Press Esc to clear, Ctrl/Cmd+A to select everything and Delete to remove what you've selected.
- **Actions at the top:** while files are selected, a bar at the top lets you select all, download or delete them. Downloading a single file gives you the file itself, not a ZIP.
- **Tidier file details:** each file shows its type, size and upload date at a glance.

## Changed
- **Videos play without sound:** screens can't start a video with sound on their own, so those videos didn't play at all. Videos now always play muted, and the "Mute video" setting is gone. Open and save older bundles that had sound turned on so their videos play again.

## Fixed
- **No more blinking images:** images no longer flash or reload when you switch widgets in the bundle editor or come back to the media library. Files you've already opened load instantly, even after you reload the page.
- **Clear error messages:** if something goes wrong when you upload, open, download or delete a file, you now get a short explanation of what happened, for example that you don't have permission or that you're offline. Before, you got a technical message or nothing at all.
- **No broken uploads:** a failed upload no longer leaves behind a file that shows up in the library but can't be opened.
