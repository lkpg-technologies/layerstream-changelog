## Fixed
- **Taking a new screenshot:** after you asked for a new screenshot, Layerstream waited a fixed 31 seconds and then said the screenshot had failed if the screen hadn't sent it yet, even though it often arrived a few seconds later. Layerstream now checks for the new screenshot every few seconds for up to 90 seconds and shows it as soon as it arrives.
