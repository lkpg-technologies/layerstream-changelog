# A new look for Layerstream

## A fresh new look
The admin has a new, cleaner design throughout: new fonts, calmer colours and more space.

- **New sidebar:** you get around with a sidebar on the left. Screens and Bundles show how many you have at a glance.
- **Phones and small screens:** the sidebar becomes a menu button at the top.
- **Organization switcher:** a tidier menu that clearly marks which organization you're in, with "Copy organization ID" at the bottom.
- **Dark mode:** looks more consistent across the app and is remembered between visits.

## Overview
A new start page that shows your screens' status, how many are online, and your most recent bundles. Click a screen to go straight to its settings.

## Screens
- **Filters and search:** filter by **All / Online / Offline**, and search by screen name or by the bundle it's showing.
- **Last seen:** hover a screen's status to see when it last checked in.
- **Updating screen:** after you change a screen's bundle, you'll see "Updating screen" until the screen confirms the change.
- **Screen settings panel:** settings now open in a side panel. From there you can:
  - see the latest screenshot or take a new one, with a progress bar while you wait;
  - rename the screen and choose which bundle it shows;
  - restart the player, or reboot the device after a confirmation step.
- **Fixed:** saving a screen's settings or restarting it could look like it had failed even though it worked.

## Bundles
- **Search and sort:** find bundles quickly and sort them by newest, oldest or name.
- **Rename in place:** rename a bundle directly in the list, with **Undo** if you change your mind.
- **Safer deleting:** before you delete a bundle, we tell you how many screens are using it.
- **Fixed:** the list now updates right after you delete a bundle.

## Bundle editor
- **New look:** the editor matches the rest of the app. The preview looks like a TV, with a grid to help you line up content.
- **Top bar:** a new top bar takes you back to Overview or Bundles. Your unsaved work is kept.
- **Your draft per organization:** each organization now has its own draft. Work in progress no longer follows you when you switch organization.
- **Dark mode switch:** you can now switch between light and dark mode right in the editor.
- **Fixed:** widgets placed against the edge of the screen now sit perfectly flush.

## Faster and smoother
- **Faster loading:** the app loads noticeably faster.
- **No jumping:** while pages load, you see placeholders the same size as your content, so nothing jumps around.
- **Messages:** notifications now appear at the bottom of the screen and can include actions like Undo.

## Small wording changes
In the screens list, "Created at" is now **Added** and "Updated at" is now **Last updated**. For bundles, "Created at" is now **Created**.
